How Shopping Works Here

Kelly’s Boutique is a members’ boutique with a personal touch. You tell us about yourself once — and then your outfits are hunted for you, piece by piece, from real secondhand listings.

One

Tell us your style

Members share their sizes, styles, favorite brands, and a budget they set themselves — along with the seasons and occasions they love. And yes, Halloween and Christmas are here year-round.

Two

We hunt the real listings

Your outfits are hunted from real secondhand listings — true prices, exactly as listed, never dressed up. The boutique’s 25% concierge fee is shown openly on every outfit, so you always see the whole picture before you decide.

Three

Try it in the Fitting Room

Take your pieces to the Fitting Room, where Pearl, our attendant, keeps the door — 6 pieces at a time, just like a real store. Our changing rooms are for everyone — women, men, and children. And for mamas, there’s a mother-and-baby room: a mama brings her infant in with her — never separated — with the baby changing spot right there, so she can try things on with her baby beside her the whole time. Inside, members see the look with their own photo before they choose.

Four

Keep what you love

Pieces you’re still thinking about wait for you on your Saved Rack. Every piece of clothing wears a security tag, removed at purchase — and your printed receipt carries the boutique’s sign and every detail, plain and honest.

Kelly’s Bundle Treat

Gathering a bundle? The first three pieces are full price — and from the 4th item on, that item and every item after it is 15% off. The treat kicks in at four and stays for the whole bundle.

Around the Store

Baskets by the door

A stack of 50 cream shopping baskets waits by the door, each one with Kelly’s Boutique on it. Take one for your trip, gather what catches your eye, and set it back by the door when you’re done.

The Fitting Room & Pearl

Pearl watches the fitting-room door with a smile. The changing rooms are for everyone — women, men, and children. For mamas there’s a mother-and-baby room, where a mama brings her infant in with her — infants stay with their mothers, never separated — and the baby changing spot is right there, so she can try things on with her baby beside her the whole time. Six pieces at a time per member — and no buying inside the room; pieces go back on the rack or out through the proper way, same as any well-kept shop.

Security tags

Every piece of clothing in the boutique wears a security tag, kindly marked “removed at purchase.” It comes off at the register when the piece becomes yours — can’t be too careful these days.

The restroom keys

Need the Ladies’ Room or the Men’s Room? Each one has its own key at the desk — just ask to borrow the one you need — and please return it at the desk at the leaving point, before you head out. The same kind rule for both rooms, and the store will gently remind you if a key is still in your pocket.

Racks in every size

Every rack in the boutique holds clothes in every size, arranged in size order — so you can walk straight to your own size and shop by your own standards, no digging and no guessing.

Shoe shelves, kept in order

The shoe shelves are kept in careful order — by size, by brand, by each shoe’s design and pattern, and by price — so the pair you’re looking for is right where it ought to be.

Tickets with the store’s sign

And every price ticket in the store carries the boutique’s own logo sign, with the piece’s size and brand printed right on it — the same way on every rack and every shelf, everywhere in the store.

Bags for the Way Home

Every purchase leaves the store in a medium Kelly’s Boutique bag — cream with plum and gold, the store’s sign printed on every one, and our thanks beneath it: “Thank you for shopping at Kelly’s Boutique — please visit again soon!”
